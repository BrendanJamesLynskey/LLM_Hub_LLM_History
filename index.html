The History of LLMs — People, Labs, Ideas

How to read it. Decks 01–03 are the technical spine — pre-transformer NLP into the 2017 paper. Read them in order. Decks 04–09 and 11 are profile decks of the labs and people that built the modern field; you can read them in any order. Deck 11 covers the producers outside the consumer-chat race — the chip vendors, open-science non-profits and enterprise builders behind the open-weight ecosystem. Deck 10 stands alone — it is the only one that is genuinely speculative, and it is intended to date.

Why a history sub-hub? Most of this site teaches the technical content of LLMs — the architectures, the kernels, the agents, the training. But the field is small enough, young enough, and weird enough that the history actually matters: the same dozen labs and the same two hundred people built almost everything you use, and the choices they made still shape what the technology can do. This series puts the names, the rivalries, and the human reasons next to the equations.
